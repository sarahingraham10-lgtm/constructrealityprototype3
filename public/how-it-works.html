How it works

Six steps, and you can see where you are at every one.

1

You design

In the VR studio, build your home. Press Send when you are happy. You will get a project reference and an access code.

2

Create your portal account

Choose your own username and password and enter your reference and code. Your design is now linked to you.

3

Architect

An architect checks your design and prepares proper drawings. A project manager reviews their work.

4

Surveyor and engineer

The land surveyor checks the real plot. The engineer designs the structure. Both are reviewed before the next step.

5

Contractor and suppliers

Contractors price the build and supply a schedule. Suppliers quote materials.

6

Your decision

Your project manager shares a proposal. You accept it, or tell us what to change.

Status you can trust

Every step shows one of: Not started, In progress, Waiting review, Being corrected or Complete. Nothing moves on until it has been reviewed.
