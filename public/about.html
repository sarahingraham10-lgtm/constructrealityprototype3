About us

Why we exist

Building a home is the biggest purchase many families ever make, yet most people agree to a plan they cannot read. They find out what it feels like only when it is built, when changes are expensive.

ConstructReality lets you stand inside your home before the first block is laid. You understand what you are paying for, and the professionals get a clear starting point instead of a vague description.

Who we are

ConstructReality was founded in Jamaica by Sarah Ingraham, a civil engineering student. We are an early-stage company building the studio and the team network, and we say so plainly. We work with licensed professionals who check and sign the technical work.

We do not claim approvals we do not have. If a drawing is a draft, it says so on every sheet.
