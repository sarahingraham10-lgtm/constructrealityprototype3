Privacy

Template. Have a Jamaican attorney check this against Jamaica's data protection law before you launch.

What we collect

Your name, username, email and phone number if you give them, your designs, messages, and files you upload. We store a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, never the password itself.

Why

To run your project: to show your design to the professionals working on it, to show you progress, and to contact you.

Who sees it

Your project manager, and the professionals assigned to your project, see your design. Each person sees only what their role needs. Your contact details are visible to the owner and your project manager, not to every professional.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information using the contact form.

Security

Sign-ins are protected, and you can change your password in the portal. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong password you do not use elsewhere.
