Questions

Short, honest answers.

What do I need to use the studio?
You design at our studio, in a Meta Quest headset, with a team member to help. Book a visit from the Contact page. Our purpose is to let you see your home under hurricane loads before building. That load testing is in development and not yet part of the studio.
Are the drawings ready to build from?
No. They are concept drafts made from your design. An architect, engineer and land surveyor must check and sign the real drawings, and your local authority must approve them.
What is a DXF file?
An editable CAD drawing. Professionals open it in AutoCAD, Civil 3D, BricsCAD and similar programs, edit it, and can save it as DWG. You also get picture versions to read.
How do I follow my project?
Create a free client account in the portal with the reference and access code shown after you send your design. You will see each step and its status.
What if I change my mind?
Change your design and send it again. The old version is marked as replaced, and your portal moves to the new one.
Who sees my information?
The people working on your project see your design and the details you give them. Your project manager sees your contact details. See our privacy page.
What does it cost?
Contact us for current prices. Contractors and suppliers give you their own quotes, which you can compare in your portal.
I forgot my password.
Contact your project manager. They can give you a temporary password, and you then choose a new one.
