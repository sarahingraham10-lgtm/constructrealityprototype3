Services

From a first idea to a priced, reviewed plan.

VR home design studio

Design your plot, rooms, floors, finishes, outside look, furniture and garden in virtual reality. Walk through at full size. Included: single and multi-storey homes, Caribbean furniture, local trees and plants.

Drawings and editable CAD files

Floor plan, electrical, lighting, plumbing, site plan, schematic foundation layout and quantities. Delivered as pictures to read and DXF files that open in AutoCAD, Civil 3D and other CAD programs for professionals to edit.

Professional review

We connect your design to architects, land surveyors and engineers who check it and add what only licensed professionals can: approved drawings, a real survey and structural design.

Rough quantities and pricing

An estimate of blocks, concrete, floor area, doors, windows and fittings, so contractors and suppliers can quote faster. Quotes are compared side by side in your portal.

Project management

A project manager is assigned to you. They review each professional's work, keep things moving and answer your questions.

Client portal

Your own login. See the status of every step, your drawings, messages, and the final proposal, which you can accept or ask to change.

What we do not do: we do not replace a licensed architect, engineer or land surveyor, and we do not give building approval. Drawings from the VR studio are concept drafts until a licensed professional signs them. Permits come from your local authority.
